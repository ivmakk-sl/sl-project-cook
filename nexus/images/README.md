Nexus page images, tracked in the repo. The background is 1300x372, the gallery images are 1920x1080. The mods-grid thumbnail is not a separate file: Nexus picks it from the uploaded gallery images. The source screenshots are 3840x2160.

- `sl_project_cook_thumb.jpg` - title card (1920x1080): the chef-hat glyph and a "Project Cook" wordmark over the dish card with its per-quality preview lines. Pick this as the mods-grid thumbnail.
- `sl_project_cook_sample.jpg` - hero shot (1920x1080): the whole cooking window with both features, the preview lines on the dish card (Country-Style Stir Fry) and the Black Rat ingredient tooltip with tier, satiety, and a negative morale value.

- `sl_project_cook_s3.jpg` - tier badges (1920x1080, title "Ingredient Tiers"): the ingredient grid with labels on a grey Low-grade badge, the game's green badge, and a gold High-end badge.
- `sl_project_cook_s4.jpg` - what is new in 1.2.0 (1920x1080, title "New in v1.2.0"): the cooking window with the Large Storage Rack as a container tab ("Storages with the Food or Cooking tag", with arrows also to the Food and Cooking tags in the cut-out tag panel "What goes in here" on the right), the open sort menu with its icons ("Sort by criteria"), the days on each item for Expiration Date ("View sort value"), and the dish card line without the quality name.
