v1.4.0 - 2026-10-23
Added: Storages tagged Cooking or Food also show as tabs in the Brewing Barrel, Rat Cage, and Ration Press windows. In the Rat Cage window you can sort them like the fridge tabs.
Added: Storage tabs in the cooking, Brewing Barrel, Rat Cage, and Ration Press windows show storage icons instead of names, so more tabs fit. Point at a tab to see its name.
Added: Dropping an item on a tab in the cooking window is easier: a drop just above or below the tab counts, and the tab's name shows above the row while you drag.

v1.3.0 - 2026-10-17
Added: From cooking level 3, control which dishes cook together by placing their ingredients in separate rows.
Added: Keep pieces of ingredients with multiple uses separate by dropping them on free cells in the ingredient grid.
Added: Uncookable items appear dimmed in cooking tabs. Fuel stays bright at stoves that burn it.
Added: Whole dish / Per portion switch beside the THIS POT title.
Added: Sort food in the Rat Cage's left panel (Backpack and fridge tabs). Satiety shows what the cage gets from all remaining uses, without talent bonuses.
Added: The Rat Cage's Food Storage grid shows the selected sort values.
Added: Store Chilled Food also fills fridges tagged Food or Cooking, then storage with either tag in the Cold Storage room while it keeps food cold.
Added: Store Chilled Food works while you stand in the Cold Storage room.
Added: The Cooking tag shows its icon above a storage, like the other tags.
Changed: Dish cards for the same recipe each show previews for their own ingredients.
Removed: Support for Survival Log versions before 1.1.18902.
Fixed: Eat values include the base stat bonuses.
Fixed: Dimmed items larger than one cell no longer show the grid lines through them (without Compact Inventory).
Fixed: The Sort button is first on the Backpack line again, before Store Chilled Food and Store Materials.

v1.2.0 - 2026-10-03
Added: Food sort for fridges and home storages tagged Cooking or Food, in the storage window and cooking window. The Backpack can also be sorted in the cooking window, and while one of these storages is open in the storage window. Sort by Satiety, Morale, Stamina, Life, Trade value, or Expiration Date without moving the stored items.
Added: Numbers on sorted items and cooking workbench ingredients: eat values per use, trade value per stack, or days left. Rotten and expired food come first when sorting by Expiration Date.
Added: Cooking tag in a home storage's Tags panel. Storages tagged Cooking or Food become cooking window tabs, usable from cooking level 2. Cooking keeps the other tags' robot rules; used alone, it works like Food.
Added: Config switches to turn off each feature (dish preview, food sort, cooking storages). All are on by default.
Changed: Dish card stats now show eat values for the whole dish, including fixed eat talents and the difficulty's Life factor. Temporary buffs, random talent extras, being tired of a food, and stat maximums are excluded. Fitness is no longer shown because eating does not change it.
Changed: Trade values in the tooltips include your appraisal talents.
Fixed: Dish card lines no longer overflow the card. The chance shows in the color of its quality level, without the name, and the text gets smaller only when needed.

v1.1.1 - 2026-09-27
Changed: Updating cooking previews requires less processing when you change ingredients.
Fixed: Long preview lines on dish cards are no longer cut off.
Fixed: The preview no longer shows too few portions when a dish's Satiety is just above the amount for one portion (for example, 40.3 Satiety with 40 per portion now correctly shows 2 portions).

v1.1.0 - 2026-09-21
Changed: Quality chances now include the cooking talents of your character.
Added: Point at a dish card to see the tier of the dish, the cooking XP for that dish, and its base trade value at each quality level. Talent bonuses that apply when you eat the dish are shown separately from the dish stats.
Added: The food badge of an ingredient shows its tier: gold with a triangle up is High-end, grey with a triangle down is Low-grade.
Added: Ingredient tooltips show the base trade value.
Improved: If a game update changes part of the cooking window, unaffected preview features can continue to work.
